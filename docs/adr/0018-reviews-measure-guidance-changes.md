# Verdict reviews measure the Evaluation Model; only Judging guidance changes it

Editors' Verdict reviews are a measure, never an override: a review doesn't change its Verdict or Live Verdict, or anything counted from them (Evaluation score, Regressions, Live score, alerts, Suggestion evidence). Letting a disagree replace the Verdict would make every reviewed Verdict agree by definition, so Evaluation Model agreement would mean nothing, and it would let an Editor clear an inconvenient Regression. Reviews improve judging in one way only: Judging guidance, general rules that the Evaluation Model may draft from disagreements, that are checked on held-out Review sample reviews and that an Owner saves. We passed on putting reviewed Verdicts into the judging prompt as examples. That would keep Visitor text outside `messages` beyond the Retention Window (a second exception beside ADR-0016), cost tokens on every judgement, and put text a Visitor wrote inside the judging instructions. Fine-tuning on reviews is deferred: it doesn't work across the providers behind the Model gateway. Don't "fix" a wrong Verdict by editing it, and don't feed reviews to the judge as examples.
