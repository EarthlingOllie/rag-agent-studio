# Suggestions point at their evidence, never copy it

A Suggestion is drawn from Visitor replies (failed Live Verdicts and thumbs-down Message Feedback) and from failed Evaluation Cases. Its evidence links to those Messages and never copies their text, so once the Purge clears a Message, the evidence reads "Text purged" while the proposed change and its explanation are kept. Copying the text would keep the evidence readable after the Retention Window, but it would add a second exception to retention beside ADR-0016's Evaluation Cases. That exception is justified because an Editor deliberately reviews and keeps each case. A Suggestion's evidence is gathered automatically and nobody chooses to keep it. The Suggestion run is also told not to quote Visitors in its explanation. Don't "fix" purged evidence by copying Message text into Suggestions.
