# 08: A Re-embed survives interruption

**Ticket:** `21-08` · **Spec:** [Job reliability](../../specs/21-job-reliability-spec.md)

**What to build:** A Re-embed whose worker died, or whose job or retry was lost, carries on from the Chunks it had not yet done, and fails cleanly if it keeps being interrupted.

**Blocked by:**
- `21-03`

**Status:** done

- [x] Re-embed jobs gain a heartbeat and claim through the job reliability module
- [x] A Re-embed with no heartbeat for 5 minutes is re-sent and resumes from the Chunks still missing the pending model's vector
- [x] A Re-embed whose first message or retry was lost starts within the queued-too-long time
- [x] Two copies of one Re-embed never embed the same batch: the second fails to claim and exits. Tests count calls on the fake embedder
- [x] A Re-embed interrupted three times with no new Chunks embedded in between fails through the existing failure path, leaving the active Embedding Model unchanged and saying why
- [x] Choosing the pending model again after such a failure starts a fresh Re-embed
- [x] Re-embeds already stuck when this ships are resumed by the first sweep
- [x] Tests cover the new behaviour at the highest seam (see [shared test seams](../../specs/README.md#shared-test-seams)); 100% line and branch coverage holds
- [x] New domain terms are added to `GLOSSARY.md`; ADRs updated if a decision changed

## Evidence

Seams: the API seam with the task seam, as the [shared test seams](../../specs/README.md#shared-test-seams) name them. A Re-embed starts when the Embedding Model is chosen through the API. The new model's provider is the scripted fake from `test_re_embedding.py`, whose `kill` step stops the job as a dead worker would, without running its error handling, and whose `calls` counts the embedding calls. A lost message comes from the Job publisher fake (`jobs` fixture). The sweeper task is called directly with the controllable clock (`clock` fixture). Outcomes are read through the Embedding Model state endpoint, the probe, the audit events and the traces. New tests: `backend/tests/integration/test_interrupted_re_embed.py` (11 tests). Run with the application changes stashed, every one of them fails.

1. **Heartbeat and claim through the job reliability module.** `backend/app/re_embedding/jobs.py` defines `RE_EMBED_ROWS` and the `RE_EMBED` policy, which `backend/app/jobs/tasks.py` adds to `POLICIES`. A re-embed job stays `running` from start to finish, so `JobTable` now accepts one status as both queued and in progress, and the claim tells them apart: a running row with no claim is queued (`JobTable._queued` and `_in_progress` in `backend/app/jobs/reliability.py`; ingestion's tables, with two statuses, behave as before). `re_embed()` claims the row with `reliability.claim`, runs inside `reliability.heartbeat`, and checks `holds()` before each batch is written, before the switch and before a failure (`_held`). Test: `test_a_live_re_embed_keeps_writing_heartbeats_so_the_sweeper_leaves_it_alone`. The provider call is held, the clock moves 10 minutes and the sweeper recovers nothing.
2. **No heartbeat for 5 minutes: re-sent, resumes from the missing Chunks.** `test_a_re_embed_whose_worker_died_resumes_from_the_chunks_it_had_not_done` uses a batch size of 1 and three Chunks. The first batch is written, and the worker dies on the second. At 4:59 the sweeper leaves the job alone. After 5 minutes it re-sends it, and the job completes 3/3 with only two more provider calls (4 in all, not 5). The probe answers with the new model, and the trace records `projects.re_embed <job id>: no heartbeat`. The job resumes because `_missing` selects only Chunks without the pending model's vector.
3. **Lost first message or lost retry: starts within the queued-too-long time.** The API sets `queued_at` when it starts a job (`service.start`). `test_a_re_embed_that_never_reached_the_queue_starts_within_fifteen_minutes`: nothing is sent at 14 minutes. At 15 minutes the job is sent and completes, and the trace says `queued too long`. Before a provider retry, the job gives up its claim and sets `queued_at` to the time the retry is due (`_queue_retry`). `test_a_re_embed_whose_retry_was_lost_starts_within_fifteen_minutes_of_its_due_time` loses the attempt-2 message. Nothing is sent at 15 minutes. It is sent 15 minutes after the 10-second retry was due, and the job completes.
4. **Two copies never embed the same batch.** `test_a_second_copy_of_a_running_re_embed_fails_to_claim_and_embeds_nothing` holds the first copy in its provider call and delivers the job again. The fake provider's call count stays at 1, and the job then completes with one call in all. Also tested: `test_a_re_embed_delivered_again_after_it_completed_does_nothing`, and `test_an_older_copy_still_running_after_a_restart_writes_nothing` (a stalled copy, given up for dead and replaced, wakes after the switch and writes nothing).
5. **Interrupted three times without new Chunks: fails cleanly.** `ReEmbedPolicy.recover` counts each interruption. The count goes back to 1 when the pending model has more Chunks than at the previous interruption (`chunks_at_interruption`). At the cap it calls `service.fail`, the same failure path the job uses: it ends the job as failed, deletes the pending vectors and records `project.re_embed_failed`. `test_a_re_embed_interrupted_three_times_without_progress_fails_and_can_be_chosen_again`: after the third sweep the job is failed with the message "Re-embedding was interrupted 3 times without embedding any new Chunks. The Project still uses multilingual-e5-small; choose the model again to retry." The Project and the probe stay on the local model, and the audit event carries the message. `test_interruptions_with_new_chunks_embedded_in_between_never_reach_the_cap`: three interruptions with progress between them, and the job completes. The cap is the setting `RE_EMBED_MAX_INTERRUPTIONS` (default 3); test: `test_the_interruption_cap_is_a_setting`.
6. **Choosing the pending model again starts a fresh Re-embed.** The same test then chooses Voyage again. A new job (a new ID, with a fresh interruption count) completes and the Project switches.
7. **Re-embeds stuck before this shipped are resumed by the first sweep.** Migration `0064_re_embed_claims` adds `queued_at`, `claim_id`, `heartbeat_at`, `interruptions` and `chunks_at_interruption` to `re_embed_jobs`. It sets `queued_at = created_at` for running jobs, which leaves them queued with no claim. `test_re_embeds_stuck_before_the_sweeper_shipped_are_resumed_by_the_first_sweep` builds that shape, started an hour ago, and one sweep completes the job. The upgrade and downgrade round trip in `test_migrations.py` covers the migration.
8. **Seams and coverage.** All the tests above run at the API seam with the task seam. The existing `test_re_embedding.py` suite still passes. Its scripted provider now waits off the event loop while it holds a call, as a real provider call awaits, so the heartbeat keeps beating. Coverage: see Gaps for the sandbox run.
9. **Glossary and ADRs.** `GLOSSARY.md` already says, under Re-embed (amended with the spec), that a job whose worker stops carries on from the Chunks it had not yet done and that one interrupted three times with no progress in between fails. No new domain terms. ADR-0020 is unchanged; this ticket applies it. `docs/development.md` gains a "Re-embeds survive interruption" paragraph under Background jobs.

## Gaps and best guesses

- **Best guess:** the job keeps its single `running` status rather than gaining a separate queued status, which would have changed the API enum, the Studio and the one-running-job-per-Project index. "Queued" is a running job with no claim.
- **Best guess:** "progress" between interruptions means the number of the Project's Chunks with the pending model's vector went up. An upload during the job, which also writes pending vectors, therefore counts as progress too.
- **Best guess:** a waiting retry stores its due time in `queued_at`, so the sweeper sends a lost retry 15 minutes after it was due. The provider-attempt number still comes from the message: a retry the sweeper sends again starts at attempt 1. Ticket 21-04 moves ingestion's attempt number onto the row, and Re-embeds can follow it.
- **Best guess:** the migration takes a running job's `created_at` as its queued time, as 21-03 took `updated_at`. A job started less than 15 minutes before the deploy is resumed by the first sweep after that.
- **Integration note:** the setting is `RE_EMBED_MAX_INTERRUPTIONS`, kept apart from 21-04's ingestion cap. Migration `0064_re_embed_claims` revises `0063_live_verdict_claims` (renumbered from 0061 at the wave-5 integration, which put the six sibling migrations in one chain, 0061..0066).
- **Local run (2026-11-26):** the backend suite ran against a local PostgreSQL 16, Redis and RustFS. Unit and live tests ran in one chunk, and the integration files ran in groups of 12 under `-n 2`, because the shared sandbox's memory limit killed larger runs. Result: 3140 passed, 1 failed. The failure was the known HNSW probe flake (`test_the_index_finds_a_projects_chunks_behind_other_projects_nearer_ones`), which passes when rerun alone. Combined coverage was 100% of lines and branches (16681 statements, 2122 branches). Ruff, ruff format, mypy and `make link-check` pass.
