# Northwind Traders product FAQ

Synthetic sample content for the RAG Agent Studio demo. Northwind Traders is fictional.

## Where do your products come from?

We import directly from small producers in Portugal, Greece and Japan, and visit each one
at least once a year.

## Are your products suitable for vegetarians?

Every product page lists its ingredients and dietary labels. Our cheeses use traditional
animal rennet unless the label says otherwise.

## How should I store olive oil?

Keep it in a cool, dark cupboard with the cap closed, and use it within three months of
opening for the best flavour.

## Do you deliver abroad?

We deliver to the UK and the EU. Chilled items ship on Mondays and Tuesdays only, so they
never wait in a depot over the weekend.
