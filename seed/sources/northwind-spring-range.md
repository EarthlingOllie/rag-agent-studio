# Northwind spring range

Synthetic sample deck for the RAG Agent Studio demo. Northwind Traders is fictional.

## Smoked paprika from Extremadura

Sweet and hot varieties, smoked over oak for two weeks. Sold in 75 g tins.

## Wildflower honey from Crete

Raw, unfiltered honey from thyme and wildflower meadows. Sold in 250 g jars.

## Yuzu kosho from Kyushu

A fermented paste of yuzu peel, chilli and salt. Keep it in the fridge once opened.
