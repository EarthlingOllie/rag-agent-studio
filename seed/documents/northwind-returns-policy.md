# Northwind Traders returns policy

Northwind Traders is a fictional speciality food importer. This policy is synthetic sample
content for the RAG Agent Studio demo; any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

## Returning an order

You can return any unopened item within 30 days of delivery. Start a return from your order
page; we email a prepaid label within one working day.

## Refunds

We refund to the original payment card within five working days of receiving the return.
Delivery charges are refunded only when the whole order comes back.

## Damaged or spoiled goods

If something arrives damaged, or a chilled item arrives warm, send us a photo within 48 hours
and we replace it or refund it in full. You don't need to send it back.

## Items we can't take back

For food safety, we can't take back opened jars, fresh cheese or chilled meat unless they
were faulty.[^1]
